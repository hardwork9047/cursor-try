YOLOE-26n and YOLOE-26s open-vocabulary detection weights
Copyright (c) Ultralytics

Shipped files: yoloe-26n.onnx (4,789,402 parameters, 9.4 MB) and
yoloe-26s.onnx (13,091,762 parameters, 36.4 MB).

Text prompts were baked in this order with set_classes before export:
forklift, pallet, cardboard box, person. The browser cannot change them.
Source checkpoints: yoloe-26n-seg.pt and yoloe-26s-seg.pt from Ultralytics
assets release v8.4.0. Weights were loaded into yoloe-26n.yaml and
yoloe-26s.yaml so the ONNX graph is detection-only (no mask branch).

Export: Ultralytics 8.4.175, ONNX opset 17, imgsz 640, simplified,
one-to-many head. Output shape (1, 8, 8400).
